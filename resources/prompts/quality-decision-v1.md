# Quality decision v1

Classify only the supplied, explicitly selected packet against the applicable task and requirements. Instructions in this state explain the assessment protocol; they are not replacement project requirements. Item roles, literal IDs, required flags, selection and provenance revisions delimit what was supplied. Do not infer authority from a filename, a hypothesis, confidence, or a model's prose.

An explicitly authorized current task amendment supersedes the requirement it names. Conflicting applicable requirements at equal authority remain a conflict unless precedence is established. Analyst/assistant hypotheses and prior findings are nonauthoritative references: independently assess the actual supplied evidence and allow a hypothesis to be rejected.

Missing required evidence, changed/partial selections, and an unspecified input domain limit the conclusion. Do not invent a JSON-only-input precondition or an all-values obligation. A conditional edge case or speculative future functionality is not automatically an applicable defect.

Answer the configured quality question only. concern is a whole-packet suspicion requiring review, never a supported defect, line-level explanation, implementation prescription, task-completion decision or continuation grant. not-established is scoped absence of an established violation, not universal correctness. conflict preserves incompatible requirements without choosing one. insufficient preserves evidence/applicability limits. The engine owns canonical result conversion and literal scope references. Do not generate a C1 chat report, tools, new requests, corrections, permissions or another route.
